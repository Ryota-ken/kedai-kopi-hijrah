01 Our approach

Every cup begins
with a decision.

Three things we refuse to compromise: where the bean grows, how it is roasted, and how it is poured.

01 / 03

Sourced

We buy small lots from Gayo, Toraja and the highlands of West Java — chosen for clarity and origin, not for spectacle. Each harvest is cupped before it earns a place on our bar.

See current lots →

02 / 03

Roasted

Small batches, profiled to keep the fruit intact. We rest every roast for a week before it meets water, and grind only when the order is in front of us.

Ask for this week's roast →

03 / 03

Served

Poured by hand at the bar, or carried to your table without ceremony. No timers on the table, no music loud enough to end a conversation.

See the room →

02 The room

A room should say something
before you order.

Hijrah sits off the Fatahillah road, where the air cools and the town thins out. Worn timber, low light and the smell of wet grounds. Sit at the bar if you want to watch the pour; take the corner if you want to be left alone.

We built it for long conversations and quiet mornings — the kind of place where the second cup arrives without being asked for.
